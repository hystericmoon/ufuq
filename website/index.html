Built for the top edge of your screen

Nothing takes space when idle. Small tiles appear only while a module has something live.

Prayer & Hijri

Kemenag, MWL, and Umm al-Qura calculations with left-wing countdowns, pre-adhan peeks, and full daily schedules.

AI Usage & Agents

Track Claude Code accounts, Codex limits, and Antigravity usage alongside live agent session status and GitHub Actions.

File Tray & Transform

Drop files onto the notch or shake a drag for a floating shelf. Convert images, compress MP4s, combine PDFs, OCR, and remove backgrounds.

Calendar & Meetings

See your next meeting with one-click Join 5 minutes before, live mic/camera indicators, mute control, and optional Auto DND.

Now Playing & HUDs

Universal playback controls in the right wing plus crisp notch capsules for volume, display brightness, and keyboard backlight.

Phone Sync

Share AI limits and agent alerts with Ufuq on Android over encrypted local-network pairing, with no cloud relay required.

New in v0.5.0

On-device utilities & Make It Yours

Start a Pomodoro cycle, keep your Mac awake during builds, monitor CPU/RAM/network rates, catch AirPods battery peeks, watch Downloads finish, and transcribe voice notes with Apple Speech, all locally.

  • Pomodoro & countdown timer with wing alerts
  • On-device Voice Transcribe (live mic + Tray audio files)
  • Stay Awake via IOKit power assertions
  • CPU, Memory, and Network throughput tile
  • Make It Yours: 5 accents, 3 glass styles, 3 notch shapes

Private by design

No analytics, no account, no cloud backend. Calendar, Microphone, and Speech permissions are requested only when you click Allow or trigger a feature.
